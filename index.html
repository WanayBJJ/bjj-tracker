Your data stays on this device

This standalone prototype stores records in your browser's local storage. It is not synchronized across devices. Export backups regularly, and don't use private browsing for permanent records.

AI coaching (future integration)

Voice dictation is available where your browser supports it. Automated transcription interpretation, verified expert-source retrieval and personalized AI coaching require a connected speech/AI service and a maintained, attributed instructional index. This version does not pretend to have those integrations.
